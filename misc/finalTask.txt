The irrigation time must be recorded for every pump operation.

Real-time transitions between the different states via the MQTT protocol.

Build a complete server-side solution with a REST API based on the existing database.

Separate routes for ESP and WEB.

- States -

Temperature Mode:
When the temperature is high, the pump operates more frequently.

Soil Moisture Mode:
The pump maintains a specific soil moisture level.

Manual Mode:
Turning the pump on and off through a user interface.

Sabbath Mode:
The pump operates independently of sensors and runs according to scheduled irrigation times.
